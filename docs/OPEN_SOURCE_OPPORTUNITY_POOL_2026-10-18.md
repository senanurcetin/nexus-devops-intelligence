## Entry rules and exit criterion

1. Do not add upstream PRs while there are **19 existing open external PRs**, absent an **explicit** maintainer-requested and owner-reviewed exception.
2. At a lower, genuinely manageable backlog, re-query issue state, related open PRs, comments/assignees and the project's *current* policy. Do not treat 2023 maintainer comments as 2026 acceptance.
3. Start **at most 1–2** independent, small, local patches, on forks **only after** scope/ownership confirmation. Require a test that fails before correction when applicable, exact SHA, reproducible build and a human-reviewed AI disclosure. Do not open upstream PRs unless separately permitted.
4. Measure success by **accepted upstream changes and maintained quality**, not opened-PR volume. No count of active users, stars, downloads or payment conversions was obtained in this audit.
5. If no candidate passes relevance/ownership and local test gating, outcome is **ZERO new patches**. That is preferable to speculative rework.
